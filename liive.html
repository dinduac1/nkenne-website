Why LiiVE

Built for people, not algorithms

Tailored to you

Your tutor works at your pace, toward your goals: a wedding toast, a grandmother's phone call, a job interview.

Human connection

Every LiiVE tutor is a fluent human being, not an AI voice. You're learning from someone who lived the culture.

Cultural immersion

Proverbs, customs, the things textbooks leave out, woven into every conversation instead of bolted on afterward.

Flexible scheduling

Book a session from anywhere in the world, whenever it fits your life: early mornings, late nights, weekends.

Phone or laptop

No special equipment. Join a session from whatever device is already in your hand.

Every kind of learner

Diaspora reconnecting with home, students going deeper, travelers, professionals, and parents raising bilingual kids.
