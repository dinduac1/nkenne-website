Why it matters

This is what Kreyol carries.

None of this is folklore. It's documented history, from the only successful slave revolution in modern history to a language born of that same struggle, still carried by every Haitian who speaks it today.

The Haitian Revolution, 1791-1804

The only slave revolt in modern history to succeed in creating an independent nation, making Haiti the world's first Black-led republic and the second nation in the Western Hemisphere to win independence.

A grammar of African resistance

Rather than "broken French," Kreyol systematically uses French-derived vocabulary within a grammatical framework tracing to the Gbe languages of West Africa and Kikongo of Central Africa.

The language everyone actually speaks

Haiti's 1987 Constitution names Kreyol and French as co-official, but Kreyol is the one language virtually every Haitian speaks, unlike French, historically the language of the elite.
