A Taste Of Sixteen

Every language says hello differently.

Here's a start. The full catalog, with LiiVE tutor availability for each, lives on the Languages page.

Southeast Nigeria

Igbo

"Ndewo"

The tongue of the Nri Kingdom and Achebe's proverbs, carrying market wisdom and ancestral memory.

Begin Igbo Southwest Nigeria

Yoruba

"Bawo ni"

Talking drums and oríkì praise poetry. A tonal language where pitch carries as much meaning as the words.

Begin Yoruba West Africa

Hausa

"Sannu"

West Africa's great lingua franca, spoken from Kano to Accra by 80 million voices.

Begin Hausa East Africa

Swahili

"Habari"

A Bantu soul with Arabic whispers, spoken from Mogadishu to Mozambique.

Begin Swahili Horn of Africa

Somali

"Iska warran?"

The tongue of nomadic poets and maritime traders. Striking rhythm, striking precision.

Begin Somali Senegal & Gambia

Wolof

"Nanga def"

The language of teranga, hospitality itself, spoken in the streets of Dakar.

Begin Wolof South Africa

Zulu

"Sawubona"

Literally "I see you." The language of clicks and kings, carrying 12 million voices.

Begin Zulu Ethiopia

Amharic

"Selam"

An ancient script all its own, spoken by 32 million people in the cradle of humanity.

Begin Amharic

Two ways to never learn alone.

The NKENNE Method

Eight ways in, one way home.

Premium lessons

15–20 minute lessons built for your commute, your kitchen, your quiet ten minutes before bed. Native speakers, not robots.

Chat with friends

Practice what you've learned, share articles, and talk with other NKENNE learners in a community built for you.

Speak Easy

Say the words out loud, correctly, without the fear. Pronunciation practice that meets you exactly where you are.

Flash cards

Grow your vocabulary the way it actually sticks. A little every day, reinforced until it's yours for good.

The blog

New essays, almost daily, on the culture, history, food, and music behind every language on the continent.

The podcast

Hosted by Progress and Blossom, with weekly conversations on language, identity, and the diaspora experience.

Offline downloads

Learn on planes, on the subway, in places without signal. Your heritage doesn't need WiFi.

Earn badges

Every milestone marked. Progress you can see, built to keep you coming back tomorrow.
